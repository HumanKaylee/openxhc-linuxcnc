# Research Sources and Provenance

## Public sources

- [LinuxCNC HAL introduction](https://www.linuxcnc.org/docs/2.9/html/man/man3/intro.3hal.html) — HAL concepts and component context.
- [LinuxCNC core components](https://www.linuxcnc.org/docs/stable/html/config/core-components.html) — LinuxCNC component boundaries.
- [LinuxCNC source](https://github.com/LinuxCNC/linuxcnc) — public GPLv2 LinuxCNC source and licensing context.
- [HIDAPI](https://github.com/libusb/hidapi) — cross-platform standard HID access library; intended under its BSD-style license option.
- [Ghidra](https://github.com/NationalSecurityAgency/ghidra) — public reverse-engineering research tool.
- [USBPcap](https://github.com/desowin/usbpcap) — public USB capture tooling.
- [Mach3 downloads](https://www.machsupport.com/downloads-updates/mach3-downloads/) — vendor software reference source, not redistributed here.
- [Remora](https://github.com/scottalford75/Remora) — external-step-generation reference for architectural comparison only.
- [17 U.S.C. § 1201(f)](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&f=treesort&fq=true&hl=false&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-2021-title17-section1201) — interoperability reverse-engineering statutory reference; not legal advice.

## Provenance rules

Public documentation records independently made observations and links to lawful public sources. It does not reproduce private manuals, decompiled vendor source, credentials, private topology, screenshots, vendor binaries, or unlicensed packet captures. Captures may inform research internally only when their license and provenance allow; they are not automatically publishable.

Every future public protocol claim must identify its evidence source, conditions, confidence, control or negative case, and exact device applicability.
