# Code of Conduct

OpenXHC is committed to a respectful, evidence-led collaboration space. Be professional, assume good intent, welcome different experience levels, and focus review on observable behavior and safety.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, publishing private information, knowingly misrepresenting evidence, or encouraging unsafe machine testing. Maintainers may remove contributions or participation that violate these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository’s maintainer contact path. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
